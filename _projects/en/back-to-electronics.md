## Where I come from

Around **1989 or 1990** I took a course in digital electronics and control microprocessors. Electronics was a different world back then. I've loved electronics since I was young, and that foundation is still there.

## Why now

The mind-driven robots sparked my curiosity again. Today there are cheap microcontrollers, every kind of sensor and a huge community sharing what it learns. It's a good time to come back.

## The plan

- Pick a robotics and electronics course and enrol.
- Record what I learn here in the log, comparing then and now.
- Put it to use in the robots project.
