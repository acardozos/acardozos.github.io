## The idea

A simple companion for moving every day. It isn't a performance app for athletes: it is a **friendly coach** that suggests routines, reminds you to do them and celebrates consistency.

Kinds of activity I want to cover:

- **Walking and jogging**, with gradual progression.
- **Gentle movement** in the style of tai chi: balance, breathing and mobility.
- **Home exercises** with no equipment, or whatever is at hand.

## Where it stands

At the concept stage: I'm defining what it should do and, above all, what it should *not* do to stay simple.

## Open questions

- How do you guide an exercise without video: by voice, large text, illustrations?
- What counts as progress when consistency matters more than speed?
- A mobile app, or a web app that works well on a phone?
