Today I launch this site. The idea is simple: to have one place to record what I do for fun, instead of leaving it scattered across folders, photos and repositories.

Software projects, electronics builds, music and audio, time outdoors and, above all, what I am learning and how I would explain it to someone else will show up here.

Writing forces you to put your ideas in order. That alone is a good first project.
